Frequently Asked Questions

Who can use PaperWise?

Any student or faculty member at your institution. Students can browse, download, vote, and request papers. Admins can upload, manage, and moderate content.

How do I request a paper that isn't uploaded yet?

Use the Paper Request feature from your student dashboard. Submit the subject and details — admins will be notified and can upload it for you.

How does the difficulty rating work?

After viewing a paper, students can vote Easy, Medium, or Hard. The community votes are tallied and shown on every paper so you know what to expect before you start studying.

Will I be notified when new papers are uploaded?

Yes. PaperWise has a built-in notification system. You get notified when new papers are uploaded, your requests are approved or rejected, new announcements are posted, and new exams are scheduled.

Is PaperWise free to use?

Yes, completely free. Create an account, browse papers, download, vote, comment, and use the AI Study Assistant — all at no cost.
